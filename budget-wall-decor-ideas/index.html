7 Budget Wall Decor Ideas for Small Apartments

In a small space, wall decor should add character without adding clutter. Fewer, larger pieces almost always look better than lots of small ones.

One oversized print

A single large print looks intentional and costs less than framing many small ones. Print your own at a local print shop.

Large framed mirror

Adds light and depth and works as decor in any room.

Picture ledge for easy swaps

Lean frames on a ledge and rearrange them without new holes.

Hang a textile or rug

A woven hanging or small rug adds texture and softens hard walls.

Plate or basket wall

Thrifted plates or flat baskets make a budget feature wall.

Framed fabric or wallpaper

A wallpaper sample or fabric scrap in a frame looks like art for very little.

Removable wall decals

Peel-off decals add pattern to rentals and come off cleanly.

Use painter's tape to mock up sizes on the wall before buying or hanging anything.
