7 Ways to Make a Rental Apartment Look Expensive on a Budget

Rentals tend to look like rentals because of a few builder-grade details. Swap those details and a small apartment looks far more expensive, and most of these upgrades leave with you when you move.

Swap the light fixtures

Builder-basic fixtures are the most obvious tell. Store the original and reinstall it when you move out, and check your lease or ask your landlord first.

Hang curtains near the ceiling

High, floor-length curtains instantly make ceilings look taller and rooms look finished.

Peel-and-stick backsplash

Removable tiles or panels cover a dated backsplash in an afternoon and peel off when you leave.

Upgrade cabinet hardware

New knobs and pulls change the look of a kitchen or bathroom for little money. Keep the old hardware in a labeled bag.

Add one oversized mirror

One big mirror looks more deliberate than several small ones and bounces light around the room.

Layer a large neutral rug

A rug that is too small is a common budget mistake. Go bigger and plainer, then layer a smaller patterned rug on top if you want color.

Plants in matching pots

Matching planters make a group of plants look styled instead of collected. Choose easy, low-light plants if your windows are small.

Take photos of everything before you change it, and keep original parts so moving out is simple.
