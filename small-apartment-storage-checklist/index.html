Small Apartment Storage Checklist: 6 Space-Saving Essentials

Small apartments rarely lack space. They lack the right storage in the right places. This checklist covers six pieces that unlock space under the bed, behind doors and up the walls, and they all work for renters.

Under-bed storage boxes

Low boxes with lids or wheels turn the space under your bed into a closet for off-season clothes, bedding and shoes. Measure the clearance before you buy.

Over-door organizer

A hanging organizer on the back of a closet or bathroom door holds shoes, toiletries or cleaning supplies without screws.

Bench with hidden storage

An entry or end-of-bed bench with a lift-up seat gives you a place to sit and a place to hide bags, blankets or shoes.

Wall-mounted drop-leaf table

Folds flat against the wall when you don't need it and opens into a desk or dining surface when you do. Ideal for studios.

Vertical shoe rack

A tall, narrow rack holds twice the pairs of a wide one on the same footprint. Put it inside a closet or behind a door.

Stackable closet bins

Matching bins use the full height of shelves and keep small items from turning into piles. Clear or labeled bins make things easy to find.

Tackle one zone at a time: bed, doors, then closet. Measure every space before buying so each piece fits.
