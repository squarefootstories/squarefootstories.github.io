Small Bedroom Makeover Checklist: 7 Steps for a Fresh Look

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 1 min read · How we write guides

A small bedroom makeover works best in the right order. Do the free steps first and spend money only on what still bothers you.

Declutter every surface

Clear nightstands, dresser tops and the floor. Empty surfaces alone make a room feel bigger.

Fix the layout first

Try the bed on different walls before buying anything. Good flow costs nothing.

Pick one calm color palette

Two or three colors across bedding, curtains and decor keep a small room restful.

Upgrade the bedding

New bedding is the fastest visual change. Layer a duvet, a throw and a few pillows.

Add a headboard or wall art

A focal point above the bed makes the room look finished.

Layer two light sources

Combine a soft overhead light with bedside lamps or sconces.

Finish with a large rug

A rug that extends past the sides of the bed softens the room and ties it together.

What to look for before you buy

Bedding

Look at fabric and care labels. Natural fibers breathe well; easy-care blends wash quickly.

Rugs

Choose a rug large enough to extend beyond the sides of the bed.

Headboards

Wall-mounted or freestanding headboards work for renters if no drilling is allowed.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Decorating before fixing the layout.
  • Using too many colors in a small room.
  • Buying a rug that only fits under the bed.

Questions people ask

Where do I start a small bedroom makeover?

Declutter, then fix the layout. Color, bedding and decor come after.

What colors make a small bedroom feel calm?

A small palette of soft, related colors usually feels calmer than many bold ones.

Take a before photo. Comparing it after each step shows which changes actually made a difference.
