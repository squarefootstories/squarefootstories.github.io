No Entryway? 7 Small Apartment Entryway Ideas That Work

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 2 min read · How we write guides

Many small apartments open straight into the living room, which means shoes, keys and coats end up everywhere. You can create an entryway with a few pieces by the door, even without a hallway. Here are seven ideas.

Wall hooks or a hook rail

A row of hooks by the door holds coats, bags and umbrellas in very little depth. If you cannot drill, look for heavy-duty removable hooks or an over-the-door rack.

A slim shoe cabinet

Tip-out shoe cabinets can be very shallow and still hold several pairs. They hide shoes and give you a small top surface for keys and post.

Floating shelf for keys

A narrow shelf at hand height becomes the spot for keys, wallets and sunglasses. A small tray or dish on it keeps things together.

A rug to mark the zone

A washable runner or doormat inside the door visually separates the entry from the living space and catches dirt from shoes.

A mirror by the door

A mirror makes a small entry feel bigger and gives you a last check before you leave. A leaning or hanging mirror both work.

Baskets for hats and gloves

A basket per person, on a shelf or under a bench, keeps small seasonal items from spreading through the apartment.

A small bench if space allows

A narrow bench gives you somewhere to sit and put on shoes, with storage below. Measure the walkway first, as a bench should not block the door's swing.

What to look for before you buy

Shoe cabinets

Check the depth and the internal height of each flap so your shoe sizes fit. Anchor tall cabinets to the wall, especially in homes with kids.

Hooks and rails

Look at the weight rating, especially for removable hooks. Heavy winter coats need stronger hooks than scarves.

Entry rugs

Washable, low-pile rugs work best by the door. Make sure the door can open over the rug without catching.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing furniture so deep it blocks the door swing.
  • Overloading removable hooks past their weight rating.
  • Letting the drop zone turn into a general clutter shelf.

Questions people ask

How do I make an entryway when my door opens into the living room?

Group hooks, a slim shoe cabinet, a shelf and a rug on the wall closest to the door. Together they read as an entry, even without walls around them.

What is the best entryway storage for renters?

Freestanding shoe cabinets, over-the-door racks and removable hooks rated for the weight you need. Check your lease before drilling.

Pick the two or three pieces your household uses most, usually hooks and shoe storage, and keep that spot only for things that come in and out the door.
