6 Small Bedroom Desk Ideas for Working From Home

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 2 min read · How we write guides

Working from a small bedroom is common, but a full desk can take over the room. These six ideas give you a proper workspace that fits the space you have, and some disappear when the workday ends.

Wall-mounted fold-down desk

A fold-down desk takes only a few inches of depth when closed. It needs secure wall mounting, so check your lease or ask your landlord before drilling.

Narrow desk under a window

A shallow desk under a window uses space that often goes to waste and gives you daylight while you work. Leave room for the curtains or blinds to move.

Closet turned into an office

If you can move clothes elsewhere, a closet can hold a desk, shelves and a lamp, and the doors close on work at the end of the day.

Corner desk in a dead corner

Corners are often unused. An L-shaped or corner desk gives a lot of surface while staying out of the main walkway.

Nightstand that works as a desk

For light laptop work, a wider nightstand or console beside the bed can double as a desk. Pair it with a small chair that tucks away.

Chair that tucks fully under

In a small room the chair matters as much as the desk. Choose one that slides completely under the desk so it does not block the path.

What to look for before you buy

Desk depth

For laptop work, a shallow desk is often enough. If you use an external monitor, allow more depth so the screen is not too close to your eyes.

Fold-down desks

Check the weight rating and how it mounts to your wall type. Some models include shelves inside, which saves wall space.

Chairs

Armless chairs tuck under desks more easily. Measure the height under the desk before buying.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying a desk deeper than your work actually needs.
  • Placing the desk where the chair blocks the door or closet.
  • Working from bed long term instead of setting up a small desk.

Questions people ask

Where should a desk go in a small bedroom?

Under a window, in an unused corner or inside a closet are usually best, as they keep the desk out of the main walkway.

What size desk fits a small bedroom?

Measure your space first. For laptop-only work, a shallow, narrow desk is often enough. Allow more depth if you use a monitor.

Pick the spot first, then the desk. A small, well-placed workspace keeps work and rest separate even in one room.
