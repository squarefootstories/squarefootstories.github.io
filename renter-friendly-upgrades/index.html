Renter-Friendly Upgrades You Can Undo When You Move

You can make a rental feel like yours without losing your deposit. These upgrades change the look of a small apartment and come off cleanly when you move. Always check your lease, and ask your landlord when in doubt.

Peel-and-stick wallpaper

Use it on one accent wall or the back of shelves. Test a small piece first, since some paint finishes can lift when removed.

Plug-in wall sconces

Add wall lighting without an electrician. Cord covers in the wall color keep the look clean.

Swap the shower head

A new shower head usually screws on by hand or with a wrench. Keep the original to reinstall when you leave.

Contact paper countertops

Removable film refreshes dated counters or shelves. Apply carefully to avoid bubbles and keep it away from direct heat.

Tension rod curtains

Tension rods hold curtains, cabinet skirts or shower curtains with no drilling.

Stick-on cabinet lights

Battery or rechargeable LED strips under cabinets add task lighting that removes without marks.

Photograph every surface before you change it, and keep original parts labeled for move-out day.
