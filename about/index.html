About Square Foot Stories

Square Foot Stories shares practical ideas for living well in small spaces: apartments, studios, rentals and tiny rooms.

Every guide focuses on changes you can actually make, most of them renter-friendly and budget-conscious. We would rather show you how to use the space you have than sell you more stuff.
