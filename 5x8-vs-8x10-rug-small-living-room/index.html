5x8 vs 8x10 Rug: What Size Rug for a Small Living Room?

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 2 min read · How we write guides

A rug that is too small is one of the most common small living room mistakes. It seems logical to buy a small rug for a small room, but a slightly larger one often makes the room look bigger. Here is how 5x8 and 8x10 rugs compare.

5x8 rug: the case for it

A 5 by 8 foot rug fits very small living rooms and narrow layouts, and sits neatly under a coffee table. It usually costs less than larger sizes.

5x8 rug: the trade-offs

In many living rooms a 5x8 floats in the middle with all the furniture off it, which can make the space look disconnected and smaller.

8x10 rug: the case for it

An 8 by 10 foot rug is usually big enough for the front legs of the sofa and chairs to sit on it. That ties the seating area together and makes the room look more spacious.

8x10 rug: the trade-offs

It needs more floor, costs more and can crowd a very small room or block doors and vents. You need to measure carefully.

Quick verdict: If the furniture legs can sit on it and some floor still shows around the edges, choose the 8x10. If not, a 5x8 under the coffee table is the safer fit.

What to look for before you buy

Measure with tape first

Mark both sizes on the floor with painter's tape and live with them for a day. You will quickly see which one fits your furniture and walkways.

Leave a floor border

A common guideline is to leave some bare floor between the rug edge and the walls, often around 12 to 18 inches in a small room.

Low pile for small rooms

A low or flat-weave rug is easier to vacuum, lets doors swing over it and looks lighter in a small space. Add a rug pad to stop it slipping.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying the rug before measuring the room and furniture.
  • Choosing a rug so small that no furniture touches it.
  • Blocking a door swing with a thick rug.

Questions people ask

Is an 8x10 rug too big for a small living room?

Not always. If the front legs of your sofa and chairs fit on it with some floor visible around the edges, it is usually the better choice. Tape it out to check.

When is a 5x8 rug the right choice?

In very small or narrow rooms, under a coffee table in a tight layout, or when a larger rug would block doors or vents.

Rug sizing is easier with tape than with guesswork. Mark it out, walk around it for a day, then buy.
