Kitchen Organization

Small Kitchen Under-Sink Organization Checklist

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 2 min read · How we write guides

The cabinet under the kitchen sink is awkward: pipes in the middle, damp risk and bottles of every height. With a short plan it becomes one of the most useful storage spots in a small kitchen. Work through this checklist in order.

Empty it and check for leaks

Take everything out and look for drips, water marks or soft wood around the pipes. In a rental, report a leak to your landlord before you organize around it.

Toss old or duplicate products

Combine half-empty duplicates and dispose of products you no longer use, following the label for disposal. Fewer bottles need less space.

Add a tray to catch drips

A waterproof tray or cabinet mat on the floor of the cabinet protects it from leaks and spills, and lifts out for cleaning.

Use a riser around the pipes

Expandable or two-tier under-sink shelves fit around the plumbing and double the usable floor space. Measure the gap between pipes before you buy.

Turntable for tall bottles

A turntable in a back corner brings bottles to the front with one spin, so nothing gets lost behind the pipes.

Hang a caddy on the door

Over-the-door caddies or small hooks hold sponges, gloves and brushes. Check that the door still closes fully once they are filled.

Lock it if kids are around

Cleaning products are commonly stored here. If young children visit or live with you, a child-safety latch is worth adding.

What to look for before you buy

Under-sink shelves

Measure the cabinet width, depth and the position of the pipes. Adjustable or expandable shelves fit more cabinets than fixed ones.

Cabinet mats

Look for a waterproof mat with a raised edge, cut to your cabinet's size. Some can be trimmed to fit.

Child-safety latches

Adhesive or magnetic latches avoid drilling, which suits rentals. Check how well they fit your cabinet doors.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Organizing around a leak instead of getting it fixed.
  • Buying a shelf before measuring around the pipes.
  • Overfilling door caddies so the door will not close.

Questions people ask

How do I organize under the sink with pipes in the way?

Use an expandable two-tier shelf that fits around the pipes, plus a turntable or bins in the corners. Measure the pipe positions first.

What should not be stored under the kitchen sink?

Avoid storing food or anything that damp would damage. Keep cleaning products out of reach of children, ideally behind a latch.

Once the cabinet is sorted, keep a simple rule: one bottle of each product, and new products only when the old one is finished.
