Kitchen Organization

7 Small Pantry Organization Ideas That Fit More

A small pantry gets messy because items hide behind each other. These ideas keep everything visible and use the full height of each shelf.

Clear airtight containers

Decant pasta, rice and snacks so you can see what's running low.

Shelf risers for cans

Step risers show back rows of cans and jars at a glance.

Door-mounted rack

A rack on the pantry door holds spices, sauces and small packets.

Lazy Susan for oils

A turntable keeps bottles reachable in deep corners.

Labeled baskets by category

Group breakfast, baking and snacks so everyone knows where things go.

Under-shelf baskets

Baskets that hang from a shelf use the gap above shorter items.

Oldest items to the front

Rotate new groceries to the back to reduce waste.

Empty the pantry once, check dates, and only buy containers for what you actually keep.
