Small Bedroom Layout Ideas: 7 Tricks to Fit More Into Less Space

In a small bedroom, layout matters more than decor. A few smart moves free up floor space and make the room easier to live in without giving up comfort.

Push the bed into a corner

Placing the bed against two walls opens up the middle of the room. It works best for single sleepers or guest rooms where access from one side is enough.

Use wall sconces, not lamps

Wall-mounted or plug-in sconces free up the nightstand and give the same reading light. Plug-in versions need no electrician.

Pick a bed with drawers

A storage bed replaces a dresser or two. Check that drawers can open fully with the bed in its final position.

Float a shelf as a nightstand

A small wall shelf holds a phone, book and glass of water without legs taking up floor space.

Hang curtains wide and high

Mount the rod close to the ceiling and past the window frame on each side. The window looks larger and the ceiling feels higher.

One tall dresser, not two wide ones

A tall chest stores the same amount on half the floor area. Anchor it to the wall for safety.

Mirror the closet doors

Mirrored or mirror-panel closet doors double the light and make the room feel twice as deep, and they replace a standing mirror.

Draw your room to scale on paper first. Moving furniture on paper is free; moving it twice is not.
