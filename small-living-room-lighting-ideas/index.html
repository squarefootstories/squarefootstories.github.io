6 Small Living Room Lighting Ideas That Make the Room Feel Bigger

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 1 min read · How we write guides

Lighting changes how big a small living room feels more than almost anything else. Light from several heights pushes the walls back and makes the room feel layered instead of flat.

Tall floor lamp in a corner

Light thrown up into a corner makes the ceiling feel higher and brightens the darkest part of the room.

Plug-in wall sconces

Wall lights free up table space and add light at eye level. Plug-in versions need no wiring, which suits renters.

LED strip behind the TV

A soft glow behind the TV reduces eye strain and makes the wall recede in the evening.

Warm 2700K bulbs everywhere

Matching warm bulbs keep the room consistent. Mixed color temperatures make a small room feel patchy.

Table lamp at sofa height

A lamp next to the sofa gives reading light where you actually sit.

Dimmer on the main light

A plug-in or smart dimmer lets one overhead light work for both bright tasks and calm evenings.

What to look for before you buy

Floor lamps

Look for a slim base and a shade that sends light up or across the room.

Plug-in sconces

Check cord length and whether a cord cover is included.

Dimmers

Plug-in dimmers work with many lamps. Check bulb compatibility for LEDs.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Relying only on the ceiling light.
  • Mixing cool and warm bulbs.
  • Placing all lamps on one side of the room.

Questions people ask

How many lamps does a small living room need?

Three light sources at different heights is a common rule of thumb.

What bulbs are best for a living room?

Warm white bulbs around 2700K suit most living rooms for evening light.

Aim for at least three light sources at different heights, and switch off the ceiling light in the evening.
