Small Space Storage

7 Hidden Storage Ideas for Small Apartments and Studios

When there's no room for another piece of furniture, the answer is hidden storage: space under, behind and above what you already own.

Storage ottoman

Works as a footrest, extra seat and coffee table, and hides blankets, games or chargers inside.

Bed risers and bins

Risers lift the bed several centimeters so taller bins fit underneath. Choose sturdy risers rated for your bed's weight.

Behind-the-door racks

Over-door racks hold shoes, towels, pantry items or cleaning supplies on space you never use.

Shelf above the door frame

A shelf mounted above a doorway stores rarely used items and books out of sight lines.

Lift-top coffee table

The top lifts to working height and reveals storage inside, so one piece covers dining, working and storing.

Wall hooks for bags and coats

A row of hooks by the door stops bags and coats from landing on chairs and the floor.

Bench with lift-up seat

An entry or window bench with storage inside gives you seating and a hidden cupboard.

Declutter first, then add storage. Hidden storage works best for things you use, not things you've forgotten.
