7 Small Bedroom Storage Ideas When You Have No Room for a Dresser

When a dresser won't fit, clothes still need a home. These ideas move storage into places a small bedroom already has: under the bed, inside the closet and up the walls.

Storage bed with drawers

A bed with built-in drawers can replace a dresser completely. Check the drawers have room to open.

Dresser inside the closet

A small chest of drawers on the closet floor uses space under hanging clothes.

Wall-mounted shelves

Shelves above head height hold folded sweaters and baskets without using floor space.

Hanging closet organizer

Fabric shelves that hang from the rod add cubbies for T-shirts and jeans.

Under-bed rolling bins

Low bins on wheels slide out easily and hold off-season clothes.

Over-door hooks

Hooks on the back of the door hold robes, bags and tomorrow's outfit.

Nightstand with drawers

Choosing a nightstand with two drawers instead of an open table adds dresser-like storage.

Declutter before buying storage. Most small bedrooms need fewer clothes more than more furniture.
