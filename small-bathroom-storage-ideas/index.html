7 Small Bathroom Storage Ideas for Tiny Bathrooms

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 1 min read · How we write guides

Small bathrooms run out of counter space first. These ideas move everyday items onto walls and doors so the counter stays clear.

Shelf above the toilet

An over-the-toilet unit or two floating shelves turn wasted wall space into storage.

Over-door towel rack

Hangs on the door and holds towels without drilling.

Shower caddy or corner shelf

Keeps bottles off the tub edge and the floor.

Drawer organizers under sink

Stackable drawers make the deep cabinet under the sink usable.

Magnetic strip for small tools

Holds tweezers, clippers and bobby pins on the inside of a cabinet door.

Baskets on open shelves

Matching baskets hide clutter and make open shelves look tidy.

Hooks instead of towel bars

Hooks hold more towels in less space and dry them just as well.

What to look for before you buy

Over-toilet shelves

Measure the toilet width and the height to the tank lid.

Shower caddies

Rust-resistant materials last longer in a humid room.

Adhesive hooks

Check they are rated for wet areas and the weight you need.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using non-rust-resistant products in the shower.
  • Overloading adhesive hooks.
  • Keeping expired products.

Questions people ask

How do I add storage to a tiny bathroom?

Use the wall above the toilet, the back of the door and the space under the sink.

What storage works in a rental bathroom?

Over-door racks, freestanding shelves and tension-pole caddies need no drilling.

Keep only what you use daily in the bathroom and store backups elsewhere.
