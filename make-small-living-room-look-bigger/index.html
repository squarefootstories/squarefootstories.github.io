Small Living Room Ideas

7 Ways to Make a Small Living Room Feel Bigger

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 2 min read · How we write guides

A small living room doesn't need a renovation to feel bigger. Most of the effect comes from where the eye goes: how high things hang, how much floor you can see and how many competing colors fight for attention. These seven changes cost little and most take an afternoon.

Raise curtains to the ceiling

Mount the rod just below the ceiling and let the panels fall to the floor. The window reads taller and the whole wall gains height. Extend the rod 15 to 25 cm past each side of the frame so the window also looks wider.

Pick a sofa with visible legs

A sofa raised on legs shows floor underneath it, and visible floor tells your brain the room has more space. Skirted or block-base sofas look heavier in a small room.

Use one large rug, not two small ones

Small rugs chop the floor into pieces. One rug big enough for at least the front legs of every seat ties the room together and makes it feel wider.

Hang a mirror opposite the window

A mirror facing the window bounces daylight deeper into the room and repeats the view. A tall floor mirror leaning on the wall works if you can't drill.

Keep the walls light and warm

Soft whites, warm beiges and pale greige reflect more light than dark colors. Pick one wall color and carry it through the room so the edges blur.

Swap side tables for wall shelves

A floating shelf beside the sofa holds a lamp, a drink or a book without taking any floor space. Fewer furniture legs make the room feel calmer.

Edit decor down to three colors

Choose a base, a secondary and one accent color, and remove what doesn't fit. Fewer colors means less visual noise, which reads as more space.

What to look for before you buy

Curtain rods

Measure from the ceiling to the floor and choose curtains long enough to just touch the floor from that height. Extendable rods let you mount wider than the window.

Sofas

Look for raised legs, slim arms and a low back. Check the seat depth and the overall width against your wall before ordering.

Mirrors

A large mirror does more than several small ones. Check the weight and use fixings rated for it, or lean it securely against the wall.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Hanging curtains just above the window frame.
  • Using several small rugs that chop up the floor.
  • Filling every wall and surface with decor.

Questions people ask

What color makes a small living room look bigger?

Light, warm neutrals on the walls usually make a room feel more open. Keeping the number of colors in the room low helps just as much as the wall color itself.

Do mirrors really make a room look bigger?

A mirror that reflects a window bounces light and a view back into the room, which makes it feel more open. Placement matters more than size.

Start with the cheapest wins: curtains, the mirror and editing decor. Then plan bigger purchases like the rug or sofa around them.
