6 Cozy Reading Nook Ideas for Small Apartments

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 1 min read · How we write guides

A reading nook doesn't need a spare room. It needs a comfortable seat, good light and a place for your current book.

Armchair in an empty corner

One comfortable chair turns an unused corner into a destination.

Window seat with cushions

A bench or low cabinet under a window with cushions makes a nook with natural light.

Floor cushions and a lamp

Large floor cushions and a floor lamp make a nook you can move or put away.

Closet turned into a nook

Remove the door from a small closet, add cushions and a sconce, and it becomes a hideaway.

Wall shelf for current reads

A small shelf or ledge keeps your books close without a bookcase.

Throw blanket within reach

A soft throw makes the spot inviting year-round.

What to look for before you buy

Reading lamps

A lamp with a directed shade or adjustable arm puts light on the page.

Armchairs

Measure the corner. A small accent chair can still be comfortable.

Cushions

Washable covers make floor cushions easier to live with.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing a dark corner without a lamp.
  • Picking a chair too big for the space.
  • Letting the nook fill with clutter.

Questions people ask

How do I make a reading nook in a small apartment?

Pick a corner or window, add a comfortable seat, a good lamp and a small shelf.

What lighting is best for reading?

A warm but bright lamp aimed at the page, rather than only overhead light.

Put the nook where you already like to sit, and make sure the light falls on the page, not in your eyes.
