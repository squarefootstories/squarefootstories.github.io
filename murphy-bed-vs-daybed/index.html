Murphy Bed vs Daybed: Best Bed for a Studio Apartment?

In a studio, the bed decides how the whole room works. Murphy beds and daybeds both solve the problem, in different ways.

Murphy bed: the case for it

It folds into the wall and frees almost all of its floor space during the day, while still giving you a full-size mattress at night.

Murphy bed: the trade-offs

It must be anchored to the wall, which can be an issue in rentals, and it usually costs more. You also make and unmake it daily.

Daybed: the case for it

A daybed works as a sofa by day and a bed by night, needs no installation, and is easy to move to your next home.

Daybed: the trade-offs

Most daybeds are twin size, so they suit one sleeper. Models with a trundle add a second bed for guests.

Quick verdict: Renting? Daybed. Own the place and can install? Murphy bed.

Check your lease before drilling into walls, and measure ceiling height and wall width before choosing a Murphy bed.
