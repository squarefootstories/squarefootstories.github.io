Round vs Rectangle Coffee Table: Which Is Better for a Small Living Room?

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 1 min read · How we write guides

The coffee table sits in the middle of every walkway, so in a small living room its shape matters more than its style.

Round: the case for it

No corners to bump into, easier to move around, and a curve softens a room full of straight lines.

Round: the trade-offs

Less usable surface for its footprint, and it can look small in front of a long sofa.

Rectangle: the case for it

Matches the shape of most sofas and gives the most surface for drinks, books and remotes.

Rectangle: the trade-offs

Corners stick into walkways, which matters in narrow rooms and homes with small children.

Quick verdict: Tight walkways or kids? Round. Long sofa and lots of stuff? Rectangle.

What to look for before you buy

Size

A common guideline is a coffee table about two-thirds the length of the sofa.

Height

Close to the height of your sofa seat, or slightly lower, is usually comfortable.

Storage

A shelf underneath or lift-top adds storage in a small room.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing a table too large for the walkway.
  • Ignoring height relative to the sofa.
  • Picking sharp corners with small children around.

Questions people ask

Is a round coffee table better for a small living room?

Often, because it has no corners and makes walkways easier. A rectangle works better with a long sofa.

What size coffee table for a small sofa?

Aim for about two-thirds of the sofa length, and leave space to walk around it.

Whatever shape you pick, aim for a table about two-thirds the length of your sofa and roughly 40 to 45 cm away from it.
