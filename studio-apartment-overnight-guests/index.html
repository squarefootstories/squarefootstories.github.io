Overnight Guests in a Studio Apartment: A Simple Checklist

By the Square Foot Stories team · Updated October 10, 2026 · 2 min read · How we write guides

A studio has no guest room, but it can still host an overnight visitor comfortably. The key is to decide the sleeping spot in advance and give your guest a small zone of their own. This checklist covers the details that matter most.

Choose the sleep spot first

Decide whether your guest sleeps on the sofa, a sofa bed, a daybed or an air mattress, and test it yourself. Check that the bed fits with a clear path to the bathroom.

Wash spare sheets and towels

Keep one clean set of sheets, a pillow and a towel ready for guests. Store them together in a bag or bin so they are easy to grab.

Clear one shelf or drawer

Give your guest a spot for their bag and clothes so their things do not spread across the room. One empty shelf, drawer or basket is enough.

Add a lamp by the guest bed

A small lamp or clip light lets your guest read or find their way at night without turning on the main light and waking you.

Plan bathroom times

With one bathroom, a quick chat about morning routines avoids a queue. Clear some counter space and a hook for their towel.

Add a screen for privacy

A folding screen, a ceiling curtain track or even a bookcase between beds gives both of you a little privacy in an open room.

What to look for before you buy

Air mattresses

A raised air mattress is easier to get in and out of than a low one. Check its inflated size against your floor space and practice inflating it before the visit.

Sofa beds and daybeds

If guests stay often, a daybed or sofa bed may be worth it. Measure the opened length and check that the mattress is comfortable enough for a full night.

Room dividers

Folding screens move and store easily. Ceiling or tension-rod curtains suit renters if your lease allows them.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Trying out the air mattress for the first time on the night itself.
  • Leaving no space at all for the guest's bag and clothes.
  • Forgetting a light the guest can use without waking you.

Questions people ask

Where can a guest sleep in a studio apartment?

Common options are the sofa, a sofa bed, a daybed or an air mattress that stores in a closet. Pick the spot that leaves a clear path to the bathroom.

How do I get privacy with a guest in one room?

Use a folding screen, a curtain on a tension rod or a tall bookcase as a divider, and agree on bathroom times in the morning.

A little preparation makes an overnight guest in a studio feel welcome rather than squeezed in. Set up the sleep spot, a small storage zone and some privacy, and you are ready.
